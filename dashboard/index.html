    Trade Decision Center · SIMULATION ONLY

    Market Analyst + News Analyst → Strategy Agent → Risk Manager → your approval → Portfolio Manager (paper ledger data/decisions.db) → Performance Analyst. Every value comes from /api/decisions.

    SIMULATION ONLY. Approve does NOT place a real order. It writes one simulated paper fill to data/decisions.db. Nothing is ever sent to an exchange, broker or wallet. Decisions use stored HISTORICAL bars (stocks: Alpha Vantage / CSV, crypto: Kraken spot), not real-time prices. Separate from the random-walk simulator and its account.
